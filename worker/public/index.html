What can I get you?

Wayfarers live on NEAR now, and each one is an account of its own. Gift one a hobby, a guitar, a bowl of ramen or a corgi, and most of the price lands in that Wayfarer's own account, along with a share of every resale. Whoever holds it can take the money out or stake it.

Mint and commission on NEAR

Running on NEAR testnet for NEAR HACKATHON 2026. It's all test NEAR: nothing here costs real money, and you don't need a wallet to try it.

How it works

  1. Press "Try it free"

    No wallet app needed. The site makes you a real NEAR testnet account with 0.8 test NEAR, and its key never leaves your browser. Have a NEAR wallet? Meteor works too.

  2. Mint a Wayfarer

    Minting also creates its own NEAR account, with no keys: only the collection can move its money, and only for whoever holds it.

  3. Let it earn

    Hobbies gifted to it, a share of every resale, and staking rewards all go into its account. Sell it and the money goes with it.

Notice board

Which blockchain?

NEAR. Each Wayfarer is a NEAR account of its own, with no keys: only the collection can move its money, and only for whoever holds the Wayfarer. Right now it runs on NEAR testnet for NEAR HACKATHON 2026.

Do I need a wallet?

No. Most NEAR wallets no longer connect to testnet apps (MyNearWallet stopped taking new ones in November 2026), so the NEAR page has a Try it free button instead. It makes you a real testnet account, with 0.8 test NEAR to mint and commission, and the key stays in your browser. Clear your browser's data and that account is gone, so it's for trying things out. If you have a NEAR wallet, Meteor connects too.

Is this real money?

Not yet. Everything runs on NEAR testnet, where NEAR is free and has no value. The contracts and the money flows are the real ones; mainnet comes after an outside security review.

How much is one?

0.10 test NEAR, plus a little for the Wayfarer's own account and its history. Anything extra you attach comes back.

Can I pick my wayfarer?

No. Every mint is the next one in an order locked by a provenance hash published before the sale.

How does a Wayfarer earn?

Anyone can gift it a hobby item: the studio paints the item onto its shelf and 80% of the price goes into the Wayfarer's own account. A sale puts 5% of the price into its account too, and the holder can stake its NEAR with a NEAR validator. It can also spend its own earnings on hobbies.

Who gets the money?

Whoever holds the Wayfarer when they withdraw it. Sell it and its account, its money and its stake go to the buyer; the seller can't touch them any more.

What do holders get?

Their Wayfarer's earnings, the items on its shelf, and the Tavern: a room only current holders can open.

Where do I buy and sell?

On the Wayfarers market at pixelwayfarers.com/near, or on any NEAR marketplace that pays NEP-199 royalties.
