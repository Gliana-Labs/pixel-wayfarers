Questions

Which blockchain is this on?

Base, an Ethereum layer 2 network. Gas costs cents, and OpenSea supports it.

How much does one cost?

About $10, paid in ETH. The exact ETH price is set on launch day and shown in the mint panel.

Can I choose which wayfarer I get?

No. Every mint is a random draw from all 1,000, including the 10 legendaries. The art order is locked by a provenance hash published before the sale.

When do I see my art?

At reveal, right after the sale ends. Until then every token shows the same mystery card.

Is the art made with AI?

Yes. Each portrait is generated from its traits with an image model, then pixelated and reviewed by hand. Bad renders get redrawn before launch.

Where can I trade them?

On OpenSea, or any marketplace that supports Base. A 5% creator royalty is set in the contract.
