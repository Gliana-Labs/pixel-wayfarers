How to mint

  1. Grab a wallet

    MetaMask or Rabby, with a little ETH on Ethereum for the mint plus gas. The mint box switches your wallet to Ethereum for you.

    Art unlocks for everyone at once after the sale, so nobody can snipe a legendary.

Notice board

Which blockchain?

Ethereum. Mint here, then trade on OpenSea.

How much is one?

0.004 ETH, about $10 at today's price, plus Ethereum gas.

Can I pick my wayfarer?

No. Every mint is a random draw, legendaries included. The order is locked by a provenance hash published before the sale.

When do I see my art?

At reveal, right after the sale. Until then every token shows the same mystery card.

What is the founders list?

Early supporters mint first, for 0.003 ETH instead of 0.004, before the public sale. Follow @pixelwayfarers, post about us on X, then sign up in the mint section with the link to your post. The same 10-per-wallet limit covers both phases.

How many can I mint?

Up to 10 per wallet, so more people get a chance at a wayfarer.

What do holders get?

Your wayfarer, and a spot in the city as it grows. Holders will be eligible for future airdrops. Details come later.

Is there a royalty?

5% on resales, set in the contract.
