Which blockchain?

Ethereum mainnet. Trade on OpenSea or any Ethereum NFT marketplace.

How much is one?

About $10, paid in ETH. The exact price is set on launch day and shown in the mint box.

Can I pick my wayfarer?

No. Every mint is a random draw, legendaries included. The order is locked by a provenance hash published before the sale.

When do I see my art?

At reveal, right after the sale. Until then every token shows the same mystery card.

Is the art made with AI?

Yes. Each portrait is generated from its traits, pixelated, then reviewed by hand. Bad renders get redrawn before launch.

Is there a royalty?

5% on resales, set in the contract.
